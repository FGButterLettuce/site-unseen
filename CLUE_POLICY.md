# Clue policy

Every clue must pass this checklist before it is published.

- Capture only public pages in a fresh, logged-out browser context.
- Never capture names, messages, addresses, avatars, account identifiers, order numbers, or other personal data.
- Prefer stable home, documentation, or sign-in pages over user-generated feeds.
- Remove embedded metadata and external image links.
- Do not reproduce private dashboards, internal tools, paywalled pages, or security-sensitive flows.
- Keep the crop transformative and limited to what the guessing game needs.
- Record the source URL and capture date in `CLUE_SOURCES.md`.
- Remove or revise a clue promptly when a rights holder raises a reasonable concern.

Community submissions should include the target answer, acceptable aliases, public source URL, three proposed crops, and confirmation that the capture contains no personal or confidential information.
